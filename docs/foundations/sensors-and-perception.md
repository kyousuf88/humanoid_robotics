# Sensors & Perception in Humanoid Robots

Humanoid robots depend on sensor fusion to understand the world.

## Core Sensors

### **1. Cameras (RGB)**
Used for:
- detection  
- segmentation  
- gesture recognition  
- scene understanding  

### **2. Depth Cameras**
(e.g., Intel RealSense D435i)

Used for:
- obstacle detection  
- mapping  
- grasping  
- human tracking  

### **3. LiDAR**
High-accuracy obstacle detection.

### **4. IMUs**
Measure:
- acceleration  
- orientation  
- angular velocity  

Critical for balance and locomotion.

### **5. Force/Torque Sensors**
Used in:
- feet (balance)
- hands (grasp control)
