# Foundations of Physical AI

This section establishes the core concepts behind embodied intelligence—AI systems that operate *within* physical reality rather than purely in digital environments.

Key topics we will cover:

- What makes **Physical AI** fundamentally different from traditional AI  
- Why **humanoid robots** play a central role in a human-centered world  
- How embodied systems perceive, plan, and act  
- The modern technical stack: ROS 2, simulation engines, and GPU-accelerated AI  
- The importance of sensing, perception, locomotion, and interaction  

Understanding these principles lets you design robots that behave naturally and safely around people.
