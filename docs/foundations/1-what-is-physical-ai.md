# What is Physical AI?

**Physical AI** is the field of artificial intelligence focused on systems that exist and operate **in the physical world**.  
These systems must understand:

- space  
- geometry  
- physics  
- constraints  
- human interaction  
- uncertainty  
- embodiment  

Unlike traditional AI models that process static data, Physical AI systems must:

1. **Sense** the environment (vision, depth, IMU, force)
2. **Understand** the scene (SLAM, detection, segmentation)
3. **Plan** an action (navigation, grasp planning, task sequencing)
4. **Act** through a physical body (motors, joints, balance)
5. **Adapt** to the real world’s unpredictability

This is why Physical AI requires a fusion of:

- Robotics  
- Machine learning  
- Perception  
- Control theory  
- Physics simulation  
- Human-robot interaction  

## Why now?

Three global shifts enable the rise of Physical AI:

### **1. Foundation Models**
Large models (LLMs, VLA models) provide reasoning, planning, and multimodal understanding.

### **2. GPU Power**
RTX-class GPUs and Jetson-based edge AI make real-time autonomy possible.

### **3. Cheap Sensors**
Depth cameras, LiDAR, IMUs, and microphones are now affordable.

Physical AI is the natural evolution of artificial intelligence.
